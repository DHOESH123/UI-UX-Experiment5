
## Aim:

To design a simple and user-friendly Airtel Super App that integrates telecom, payment, banking, bill, and customer support services in one platform.
### Algorithm

1. **Start**
2. Login and verify the user.
3. Display the **Home Dashboard**.
4. User selects a service: Recharge, Bills, UPI, Bank, Loan, or Support.
5. Process the selected service.
6. Confirm the transaction/request.
7. Display the **success/failure status**.
8. Save the transaction/request in history.
9. Return to Home or Logout.
10. **Stop**.
## Problem statement:

**PROBLEM STATEMENT:
Problem Statement – Airtel Super App
Title: Improving the User Experience of a Unified Telecom, Banking, Payment, and Customer Support Application

Modern users depend on mobile applications for recharging, bill payments, banking, financial services, transaction management, and customer support. However, managing these services can become complicated when users have to navigate through multiple sections, remember different payment details, track requests, and search for the right service.
The proposed Airtel Super App aims to provide a single, user-friendly platform that integrates telecom services, financial services, UPI payments, bill payments, loan-related services, and customer support. The application should reduce complexity and provide users with quick access to their most frequently used services.
Major Problems to Address
1.Fragmented Services 
1.Users need access to multiple services such as mobile recharge, Wi-Fi, broadband, banking, cards, investments, and payments. 
2.These services can be difficult to manage when they are not presented in a simple unified interface. 
2.Complex Navigation 
1.A large number of services can make the application difficult to navigate. 
2.Users may spend unnecessary time searching for specific features such as recharge, bill payment, or transaction history. 
3.Difficulty Tracking Financial Activities 
1.Users need an easy way to monitor payments, recharges, bills, loans, and other financial activities. 
2.Important information such as upcoming repayments or pending payments should be clearly visible. 
4.Loan and EMI Management 
1.Users may forget loan repayment dates or EMI-related information. 
2.The application should provide clear reminders and easy access to repayment-related services. 
5.Multiple Payment Requirements 
1.Users frequently perform different transactions such as electricity bills, mobile recharge, loan EMI payments, and UPI payments. 
2.Switching between different services for each transaction creates unnecessary effort. 
6.Customer Support Difficulties 
1.Finding the appropriate customer-support option can be time-consuming. 
2.Users may not know whether their issue is related to their phone, network, payment, or another service. 
7.Poor Request Tracking 
1.After raising a complaint or service request, users need to know its current status. 
2.Lack of clear tracking can create uncertainty and result in repeated support requests. 
8.Information Overload 
1.Combining telecom, banking, cards, investments, payments, and support in one application can overwhelm users. 
2.The interface needs to prioritize important information without making the screen cluttered. 
9.Personalized Service Access 
1.Different users use different services frequently. 
2.The application should make frequently used services easily accessible and reduce unnecessary steps. 
10.Need for a Simple and Consistent Interface 
All major services should follow a consistent design and interaction pattern. 
Users should be able to understand icons, categories, buttons, and navigation without confusion. **
